What is a Robotics Technician?

A Robotics Technician works with robotic systems by building, testing, repairing, and maintaining them. They help ensure that automated systems and machines operate properly in industries such as construction, manufacturing, and engineering.

Unlike robotics engineers, technicians focus more on the practical and hands-on side of robotics systems.

Where Robotics Technicians Work

  • Manufacturing facilities
  • Automation companies
  • Engineering and construction projects
  • Warehouse robotics systems
  • Maintenance and repair facilities

Technologies Used

  • Industrial robotic arms
  • Sensors and control systems
  • Embedded computer systems
  • Mechanical and electrical tools
  • Automation software

Important Skills

  • Troubleshooting and problem solving
  • Mechanical and electrical repair skills
  • Understanding of robotics systems
  • Basic programming knowledge
  • Attention to detail

Education Pathway

Most robotics technicians complete:

  • College diploma programs
  • Technical certifications
  • Apprenticeships or co-op training

This career provides a more hands-on pathway into robotics and automation without requiring a university engineering degree.
