What is an ASIC Design / Verification Engineer?

An ASIC engineer designs, verifies, and optomizes custom silicon chips for specific high-performance applications like AI, networking, or automative systems

Verification engineers ensure that these chips function correctly by testing their design before they are manufactured.

What They Work On

  • Smartphones and mobile processors
  • Graphics processing units (GPUs)
  • AI and machine learning chips
  • Automotive control systems
  • Medical devices and embedded systems

Technologies Used

  • Hardware Description Languages (Verilog/VHDL)
  • Logic simulation tools
  • Computer architecture design
  • Universal Verification Methodology
  • Digital circuit design

Important Skills

  • Strong understanding of digital electronics
  • Computer architecture knowledge
  • Programming (C, SystemVerilog, Python)
  • Problem solving and debugging
  • Attention to detail (critical for hardware testing)

Education Pathway

Most ASIC engineers require a university degree in:

  • Computer Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Microelectronics Engineering

Career Importance

ASIC engineers are essential in the development of modern computing technology. Every smartphone, computer processor, and AI system relies on custom chip designs created and tested by engineers in this field.

Salary can range from $87,000 - $133,000 per year in Canada
