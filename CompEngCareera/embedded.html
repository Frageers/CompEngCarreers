What is an Embedded Systems Engineer?

An Embedded Systems Engineer designs and builds small computer systems that are built directly into machines and devices. These systems are not visible like a laptop or desktop computer, but they control how machines operate.

They combine hardware and software engineering to create efficient, reliable systems used in everyday technology and industrial machines.

Where Embedded Systems Are Used

  • Robotics and automation systems
  • Cars and autonomous vehicles
  • Medical devices (heart monitors, scanners)
  • Construction machinery and sensors
  • Smart home devices (thermostats, security systems)

Technologies Used

  • Microcontrollers (Arduino, ARM processors)
  • Embedded programming (C, C++)
  • Real-time operating systems (RTOS)
  • Sensors and actuators
  • Circuit design and electronics

Important Skills

  • Strong programming ability (especially C/C++)
  • Understanding of electronics and circuits
  • Problem solving and debugging hardware/software systems
  • Knowledge of microprocessors and control systems
  • Attention to detail

Education Pathway

Most embedded systems engineers study:

  • Computer Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Mechatronics Engineering

Salary can range from $90,000 - $116,000 per year in Canada
