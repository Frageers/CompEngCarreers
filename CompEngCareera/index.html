What is Computer Engineering and other key terms

Computer engineering is a field that combines computer science and electrical engineering to develop, design, and test computer hardware, and software.

Computer science is the study of computers and algorithmic processes focusing on how they operate and they're application to solve complex real world problems.

Electrical engineering is the branch of engineering that applies the math of electricity, and circiuits to design, develop, and test electrical systems and components

Why Computer Engineering is Important

Computer engineering is the bridge between hardware and software. By combining computer science with electrical engineering, the development of everything from processors for smartphones to fully autonomous vehicles is enabled

Computer Science gives us the algorithm behind the AI, what lets it think

Electrical engineering gives us a way to power the circuit, a working circuit board, and a way to make sure the circuit doesn't overheat or explode

Computer engineering combines the 2, designing the working circuit in a way to allow for the input of information to the algorithm and to output the processed information from the algorithm

Technologies Used

  • Embedded systems used in robotics and construction machinery
  • Sensors and microcontrollers used in smart infrastructure
  • Computer-Aided Design (CAD) used to create digital building models
  • Simulation software used to test structures before construction

Careers Enabled by These Technologies

Computer engineering supports many careers in science and construction fields. Some of the careers explored on this website include:

  • CAD Technician
  • Robotics Technician
  • GIS Analyst
  • Construction Technology Specialist
Key Idea: Computer engineering connects hardware, software, and electronics to create the tools that allow scientists and engineers to design, test, and build modern infrastructure.
